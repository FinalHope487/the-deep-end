Source: user answers Q5-Q8, Q21-Q26 in `docs/handoff/core-aspect-answers-2026-11-05.md`. Options: `docs/handoff/question-bank-2026-11-05.md`.

| ID | Decision |
|---|---|
| Q21 | Side routes have dead ends sometimes. Put a small thing to see near each dead end |
| Q7 | Ordinary pools: shallow and deep water are both common. User note: this does not limit flooded rooms and other special areas. Those areas keep the water level from their own rule |

| Q8 | Normal stairs carry the main use. Special stairs and slides appear sometimes, and a usable one really works |

| Q23 | Calm water with soft ripples |

## Basis

- The Q7 option B does NOT mean every room is half shallow and half deep. Keep two cases: ordinary pool rooms (mix rule) and special flooded spaces (own rule).
- Keep the earlier rules: deep water is not enterable, photo water levels (pool5, ppool12), diving board safety limit (Q4 in `space-round2-answers-2026-11-05.md`).

## Replaces and adds

- Adds to `docs/spec.md` §2.2.4 and §3.1 for v3 only. Removes no earlier rule.

## Cost to reverse

Changes the water, facility and route strategy of the new generator. After the generator exists, it can change world shapes and routes. No save change.
