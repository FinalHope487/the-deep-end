### POOLS

- Kind, creator, year: game; Tensori; 2024
- Tags and basis: 1,2,5; a: the developer names Poolrooms, Backrooms and liminal spaces as direct inspiration. The store text presents exploration as both relaxing and unsettling.
- Status: released (2024-04-26; later releases on more platforms)
- Theme: familiar swimming facilities become strange when people and normal activity are absent. Relaxation, isolation and fear of getting lost.
- Flow: the player walks through connected spaces, looks and listens. No missions, dialogue or chasing enemies. Navigation is the main activity. Six chapters; each has a different environment and mood.
- Content outline: empty swimming facilities and related architectural spaces. Some areas are mazes. The world changes as the player progresses. Environmental sound replaces a narrative interface.
- Art style and rendering: detailed, realistic interiors. Tile, water and room scale make the visual identity. Familiar materials in unusual arrangements.
- Lighting and colour: white or pale tile, blue water, contrast between bright and dark spaces. Light changes the apparent depth and safety of rooms.
- Camera and media texture: first person. Water movement and room-dependent echo. The developer names found-footage imagery as an inspiration. No lens model is shown in sources.
- Space composition and scale: large pool halls connect to narrow passages, repeated rooms and less familiar structures. Open and confined spaces alternate. Exploration is more important than puzzles.
- Sources: https://store.steampowered.com/app/2663530/ (O); https://www.pcgamer.com/games/adventure/games-industry-should-be-less-cautious-says-maker-of-experimental-game-about-exploring-a-swimming-pool-dimension/ (M)
