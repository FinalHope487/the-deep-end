### Antichamber

- Kind, creator, year: game; Alexander Bruce; 2013
- Tags and basis: 4; a: store text "Escher-like world where hallways wrap around upon each other, spaces reconfigure themselves"
- Status: released (2013-01-31, PC)
- Theme: perception and the player's assumptions about space. Each solved rule teaches a short life lesson on a wall sign.
- Flow: non-linear maze. A map room shows found areas; the player can return to it at any time. A wall clock counts down about 90 minutes; the end does not depend on the clock.
- Content outline: corridors that change when the player looks back, stairs that lead to a different place when the player goes up or down, and a block gun that moves coloured cubes. Puzzles open the routes to the end chamber.
- Art style and rendering: Unreal Engine 3. Flat white surfaces with thin black edge lines. Saturated flat colours (red, green, blue, yellow) mark rules and cubes. Almost no texture.
- Lighting and colour: even, mostly shadowless light. Colour carries meaning: a room colour often marks a puzzle zone.
- Camera and media texture: first person. Clean digital image. No lens effects in the store images.
- Space composition and scale: small rooms and narrow corridors in a graph that is not Euclidean: the same corridor gives a different place on a second walk; a fall can land in a room above. Scale is human, but routes are not consistent.
- Sources: https://store.steampowered.com/app/219890/ (O)

### Manifold Garden

- Kind, creator, year: game; William Chyr Studio; 2019
- Tags and basis: 4,5; a: store text "Escher-esque world of impossible architecture. Witness infinity in first-person"
- Status: released (2019-10-18 Epic and Apple Arcade; 2020-10-20 Steam)
- Theme: infinity, order and growth. Calm contemplation of architecture.
- Flow: linear series of worlds from a hub. Each world ends when the player grows a garden that opens the next path.
- Content outline: the player can turn any wall into the floor (six gravity directions). Coloured cubes match their gravity colour and only move in that frame. Water and trees are parts of puzzles.
- Art style and rendering: Unity. Brutalist block buildings, clean geometry, strong edge lines. Distance fog with colour.
- Lighting and colour: each gravity direction has its own colour on walls and cubes. Light, pale air with tinted fog; world sets change the main hue.
- Camera and media texture: first person. Clean image; materials are flat concrete and water planes.
- Space composition and scale: the world repeats without end in all three axes. If the player falls off an edge, they fall back into the same world from above. The repeated copies make very large and very far views from a small source volume.
- Sources: https://store.steampowered.com/app/473950/ (O)
- Images: steam-473950-1.jpg; steam-473950-2.jpg

### Superliminal

- Kind, creator, year: game; Pillow Castle Games; 2019
- Tags and basis: 4; a: store text "surreal dream world ... ambiguity of depth and perspective"
- Status: released (2019-11-12 Epic; 2020-11-05 Steam)
- Theme: a dream-therapy programme. The player is in a dream and must wake up. The voice of a doctor guides the player.
- Flow: linear chapters. Each chapter is a dream level with a new trick.
- Content outline: forced perspective: an object picked up keeps its screen size, so its real size changes with distance. Other tricks: painted shapes that become objects from one view, doors that repeat, copies of objects.
- Art style and rendering: realistic office, hotel and home interiors with clean surfaces. Later chapters become darker and more abstract.
- Lighting and colour: soft indoor light at the start (fluorescent office, warm hotel). One later chapter is very dark and uses horror tension.
- Camera and media texture: first person. Clean image. Materials: carpet, ceiling tiles, painted walls.
- Space composition and scale: ordinary rooms whose scale changes with the player's view. Room sequences repeat as loops. Scale distortion is the main tool.
- Sources: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1049410/ (O)
- Images: steam-1049410-1.jpg; steam-1049410-2.jpg

### Viewfinder

- Kind, creator, year: game; Sad Owl Studios; 2023
- Tags and basis: 4; a: store text "reshape the world around you with an instant camera"
- Status: released (2023-07-18)
- Theme: rebuilding a lost world from pictures; a story about a research group and a climate crisis.
- Flow: hub levels with sets of short puzzle levels. Each level ends at a teleporter.
- Content outline: the player holds a photo, drawing or painting up to the view. The picture becomes 3D space at that place and cuts away what was behind it.
- Art style and rendering: bright stylised 3D. Some levels show pictures in other styles (drawings, paintings) that become geometry in that style.
- Lighting and colour: sunny and saturated in most levels. Sky and pastel colours.
- Camera and media texture: first person. The instant camera and printed photo frame are the main media device.
- Space composition and scale: small floating islands and platforms. A picture can add a bridge, a room or a large building in one step. Scale changes with the picture angle and distance.

### NaissanceE

- Kind, creator, year: game; Limasse Five (Mathieu Boulard); 2014
- Tags and basis: 4; a: store text "first person exploration game ... a primitive mysterious structure"
- Status: released (2014-02-13, free on Steam)
- Theme: a lost being inside a huge structure. Wonder and a feeling of being very small.
- Flow: mostly linear path through large zones, with platform and light-and-dark sections. A breath key controls running and stamina.
- Content outline: no text story. The player walks, climbs, falls and finds exits in light. Some sections need precise jumps in the dark.
- Art style and rendering: Unreal Development Kit. Black and white abstract geometry: blocks, columns, shafts. Almost no texture.
- Lighting and colour: monochrome. Strong contrast between full white light and full black. Light is the guide and sometimes the danger.
- Camera and media texture: first person. Clean image with heavy bloom in light areas.
- Space composition and scale: megastructure. Very tall shafts, long empty halls, repeated columns. The scale is far larger than the player; voids and drops make the scale clear.
- Sources: https://store.steampowered.com/app/265690/ (O)

### Hyperbolica

- Kind, creator, year: game; CodeParade; 2022
- Tags and basis: 4; a: store text "Non-Euclidean adventure ... lines can never be parallel, horizons are curved, and space grows exponentially"
- Status: released (2022-03-14)
- Theme: play with hyperbolic and spherical geometry in a light, friendly world.
- Flow: hub world with several areas, quests and mini-games. Collect items to go on.
- Content outline: towns, mazes, a snow world, a forest, puzzles and a camera to take photos. Some areas use spherical space where far objects grow.

### Myst

- Kind, creator, year: game; Cyan (Robyn and Rand Miller); 1993
- Tags and basis: 4; c: MoMA collection text + The Strong museum
- Status: released (1993 Mac; realMyst 2000; Myst 2021 full real-time remake)
- Theme: a deserted island and the books that link to other worlds (Ages). Two brothers trapped in books.
- Flow: open exploration of the island; the player opens four Ages in any order, then chooses an ending.
- Content outline: machines, levers and codes. Clues are in journals and in the space itself. No inventory pressure, no death.
- Art style and rendering: 1993: pre-rendered 3D still images on Mac (HyperCard). 2021: real-time 3D with realistic materials.
- Lighting and colour: natural daylight on the island; each Age has its own palette (dark forest, mechanical red, water blue, wind-swept stone).
- Camera and media texture: 1993: one still image per node; the player clicks to step to the next image. 2021: free first-person movement, also VR.
- Space composition and scale: small island with distinct landmarks. Each Age is a compact space with one main structure. Routes connect through linking books, not through walking.
- Sources: https://www.moma.org/collection/works/164918 (O); https://www.museumofplay.org/games/myst/ (O); https://store.steampowered.com/app/1255560/ (O)

### Monument Valley

- Kind, creator, year: game; ustwo games; 2014
- Tags and basis: 4; a: ustwo case study (a game about architecture)
- Status: released (2014-04-03 iOS; PC 2022)
- Theme: a silent princess, Ida, walks through monuments and returns sacred geometry. Forgiveness.
- Flow: ten short chapters. Each chapter is one monument puzzle.
- Content outline: the player rotates and slides parts of the building to join paths. Crow People block routes. A totem friend helps.
- Art style and rendering: isometric 3D with flat colours and simple shapes. Influence from Escher and Japanese prints.
- Lighting and colour: pastel palettes per chapter; soft gradients; strong flat shadows.
- Camera and media texture: fixed isometric view. Paths connect when they line up on the screen, not in 3D.
- Space composition and scale: impossible objects (Penrose shapes). One small monument per screen. Rotating a part changes which paths connect.
- Sources: https://ustwo.com/work/monument-valley/ (O); https://store.steampowered.com/app/1927720/ (O)
