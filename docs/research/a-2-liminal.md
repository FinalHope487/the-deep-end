### The Exit 8

- Kind, creator, year: game; KOTAKE CREATE; 2023
- Tags and basis: 2,4; a: the creator names Japanese underground passages, liminal spaces and the Backrooms as direct inspirations. Repeated passage and observation-based navigation make space central.
- Status: released (2023-11-29; more than 3 million copies sold by 2026-09)
- Theme: an ordinary Japanese underground passage becomes an endless loop. Small changes in a familiar place create uncertainty.
- Flow: observe the passage, then go on or turn back. The correct choice depends on whether an anomaly is present. A correct choice advances the exit counter; a wrong one resets it. Goal: reach Exit 8.
- Content outline: a repeating passage with signs, advertisements, doors and everyday objects. Some details change between loops; the player must notice them to escape.
- Art style and rendering: realistic Japanese transit architecture with restrained detail. Ordinary surfaces, signs and objects; no fantasy structures.
- Lighting and colour: bright overhead light and pale walls, like a normal public space. Constant light helps the player compare one loop with the next.
- Camera and media texture: first person. Adjustable camera shake and mouse sensitivity. VHS effects and lens model not shown in sources.
- Space composition and scale: one short repeated corridor (an L-turn loop). The controlled route makes small differences easy to miss. Repetition gives an endless place without a large map.
- Sources: https://store.steampowered.com/app/2653790/ (O); https://awards.cesa.or.jp/2024/en/year/prize/14.html (O); https://automaton-media.com/en/interviews/interview-the-exit-8-developer-kotake-create-on-the-perks-of-being-a-solo-dev-and-how-platform-8-came-to-be/ (M); https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/the-exit-8-sales-top-three-million (M)
- Images: steam-2653790-1.jpg; steam-2653790-2.jpg

Note (8-likes): the observation-and-loop structure started a group of games called "8-likes" (8番ライク). KOTAKE CREATE discussed the group and named False Dream and other examples in a 2024 interview. This is an influence note, not one combined work and not a list of entries.
